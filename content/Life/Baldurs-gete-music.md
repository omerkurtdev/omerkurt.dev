Baldur’s Gate 3 has completely immersed me in its world, making me feel as if I've left reality behind. The depth of each character and the impact of my choices make the game feel incredibly personal. Character creation itself is an adventure, and every battle demands strategic thinking, which keeps the experience fresh and engaging.

The game’s dark and mysterious atmosphere is captivating, while the music, with its intense and emotional tones, enhances the entire experience. I can’t forget to mention Minachua’s Turkish covers — they’re truly amazing, and I’ll be sharing a couple of them below.
